# oviedo_refuel_easy

Controlled refuelling scenario.

- **EV_LOW** starts at URIA below 25% charge and must charge at CALATRAVA before serving R_EV1.
- **ICE_LOW** starts at URIA below 25% fuel and must refuel at ESTACION before serving R_ICE1.
- **R_EV2** checks that the EV keeps working after charging.
